[edit] layout_left_padded and layout_right_padded are in the navigation bar but missing in the class template documentation

These are new in C++26, but I see submdspan and submdspan_extents both in the nav bar and the documentation. Is this an omission or just a matter of time?
ticotico (talk) 10:22, 27 June 2024 (PDT)

All of this will obviously need to be added to this site over time.)
  • submdspan and submdspan_extents are both from P2630R4 - Submdspan and are already in the C++26 draft:
    • [mdspan.sub] and
    • [mdspan.sub.extents].
  • layout_left_padded and layout_right_padded are from Padded mdspan layouts - P2642R5 and are as well in the draft:
    • [mdspan.sub.map.leftpad] and
    • [mdspan.sub.map.rightpad].
--Space Mission (talk) 21:00, 27 June 2024 (PDT)
