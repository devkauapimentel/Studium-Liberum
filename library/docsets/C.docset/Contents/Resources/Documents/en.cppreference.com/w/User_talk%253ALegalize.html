The default

Latest edit reminds me, the default {{no|}} may not be the best idea here: many individual language features are not listed by compiler vendors in their published support matrices, and unless someone actually tests, it's hard to tell which version introduced support for such. --Cubbi (talk) 21:57, 26 June 2015 (PDT)

I agree, perhaps {{n/a}} could work? --P12 19:47, 7 August 2015 (PDT)
I assume you're talking about the default value for the compiler support row template? It seems safest to me to assume that the feature isn't present unless they say they support it. If that isn't accurate enough for your tastes -- it's your wiki, I'm just a contributor -- then I would prefer some way of indicating "unknown" instead. "n/a" sounds just as authoritative as "no" when you don't know if it applies or not. --Legalize (talk) 16:12, 10 August 2015 (PDT)
